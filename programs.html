Programs designed to empower

Four connected programs help women and girls withstand shocks, rebuild their lives and lead change in their communities.

Program 01
Livelihoods & savings groups

Income that women control

We help women save, borrow and grow small businesses so they can feed their families, pay school fees and cope with shocks.

  • Village savings and loan associations run by women
  • Business skills, bookkeeping and market linkages
  • Climate-smart farming, seeds and tools
  • Start-up grants for women-led enterprises
120savings groups supported
Program 02
Peacebuilding & dialogue

Women at the peace table

We train women as mediators and support peace committees that resolve disputes over land, cattle and resources before they turn violent.

  • Women mediators and community peace committees
  • Dialogues between youth, elders and leaders
  • Early warning of local tensions
  • Trauma healing and social cohesion activities
45peace committees active
Program 03
Girls' education & skills

Keeping girls in school

We remove the barriers that push girls out of school and give young women practical skills that lead to work.

  • School fees, uniforms, books and dignity kits
  • Mentoring and girls' clubs
  • Vocational training in tailoring, solar and phone repair
  • Family outreach to support girls' education
2,100girls supported
Program 04
Protection & health

Safety, care and dignity

We help survivors of gender-based violence get support and work with communities to prevent violence and improve mothers' and children's health.

  • Safe referral to health, legal and psychosocial services
  • Community awareness on preventing violence
  • Maternal and child health and nutrition sessions
  • Women-friendly spaces and support groups
3,800women reached
Across all programs

Themes that run through everything

These priorities shape how every WIRCO program is designed and delivered.

Climate resilience

Preparing families for floods and drought with better farming, savings and early action.

Inclusion

Reaching women with disabilities, widows, displaced women and young mothers.

Emergency response

Food, water and shelter support when crises hit the communities we serve.
