Featured story

How one savings group changed a village

When twenty women in a flood-affected village began saving a little each week, few people expected much. Two years later the group runs a grain store, lends to new members and has paid school fees for dozens of children.

Their success has inspired three more groups in neighbouring villages, each led by women who first joined as members.
