Measuring what really changes

We measure our impact not only by how many people we reach, but by what changes in their lives.

Our approach to impact

Beyond the numbers

WIRCO's impact will be measured not only by how many people are reached, but also by what changes for them, including increased knowledge, skills, confidence, access to services, economic opportunities, safety, participation, leadership and the ability to exercise their rights.

Knowledge Skills Confidence Access to services Economic opportunities Safety Participation Leadership Exercising rights
How we measure

Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning

Our MEAL approach helps us keep our programmes effective and accountable to the communities we serve.

Monitor

Regularly monitoring programme activities and progress.

Measure

Measuring results against established objectives and indicators.

Listen

Collecting feedback from programme participants.

Learn

Using lessons learned to improve programme quality and effectiveness.

Strategic Plan 2026–2031

Our hopes for 2031

By 2031, WIRCO seeks to contribute to communities in which children, women and girls are safer, more economically empowered and better represented in leadership; people with disabilities experience greater dignity, accessibility and participation; vulnerable families have stronger pathways to basic needs and self-reliance; young people have meaningful opportunities to lead; and communities are better equipped to prevent violence, respond to crises, promote health and advance human rights.

Read the strategic plan

Our seven expected outcomes

  • Economic and social empowerment
  • Basic needs, food security and community resilience
  • Safety, protection, legal access and crisis support
  • Holistic health, mental health and wellbeing
  • Youth, girls and intergenerational leadership
  • Inclusion, disability rights and meaningful participation
  • Stronger communities, partnerships and systems
