Faith-based, women-led, community-centred

The Women-Led Initiative for Resilient Communities (WIRCO) is a faith-based, women-led national non-governmental organisation serving vulnerable and marginalised people across South Sudan.

Our story
Our story

It began with an act of compassion

WIRCO's story began when the late Mrs. Rujalba Ayang met a divorced mother of two, living with a disability, who had collapsed from hunger. Moved by her situation, Mrs. Rujalba spoke to the women of Imolongo Catholic Church in Omeo Boma, who came together to provide food, shelter, medicine and psychosocial support.

Mrs. Rujalba led the movement that followed. WIRCO began in 2023 as a small church-based charity and has since become a registered national non-governmental organisation with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC Reg. No. 6647).

In tribute to Mrs. Rujalba, who has since passed away, our primary focus has always been the health and nutrition of malnourished children. Because children's nutrition is closely connected to the wellbeing of families and communities, our work also addresses the wider needs that affect people's health, safety, opportunities and ability to support their households.

Our vision

Resilient and inclusive communities in South Sudan where all people, especially children, women, girls, older persons and other vulnerable groups, can live healthy, safe and dignified lives.

Our mission

To work with communities in South Sudan to improve the wellbeing and resilience of vulnerable and marginalised people through nutrition and health support; water, sanitation and hygiene; women's and girls' empowerment; food security and livelihoods; gender and protection; education; and environmental conservation.

Our faith

Rooted in faith

As a faith-based organisation, WIRCO lives out its Christian values alongside its humanitarian and development work. Following the Great Commission in Matthew 28:18–20, we give programme participants and communities the opportunity to know God's kingdom and the teachings of Jesus Christ.

Our values

What guides our work

These values guide our conduct and behaviour as we work towards our vision.

Compassion

Showing love, care and concern for vulnerable and marginalised people, especially malnourished children, women and girls.

Human dignity

Respecting the worth, rights and dignity of every individual, regardless of their background.

Empowerment

Promoting the independence, participation and opportunities of women, girls and vulnerable communities.

Equality and inclusion

Ensuring that everyone, including older persons and marginalised groups, has equal opportunities and access to support.

Integrity and accountability

Being honest, transparent and responsible in managing organisational resources and serving communities.

Community participation

Working together with communities and respecting their needs, priorities and contributions.

Resilience

Strengthening the ability of individuals, families and communities to overcome challenges and recover from difficulties.

Sustainability

Promoting environmental conservation, food security and livelihoods for long-term community wellbeing.

Protection and safety

Promoting the safety, rights and wellbeing of children, women, girls and other vulnerable groups.

Faith and service

Demonstrating our faith-based commitment through service, love and support for people in need.

Our objectives

What we set out to achieve

  • To improve child nutrition and health by providing support and services to malnourished children and vulnerable families.
  • To promote access to clean water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) to improve community health and prevent water-related diseases.
  • To empower women and girls through education, skills development, participation and opportunities for economic independence.
  • To promote food security and sustainable livelihoods by supporting vulnerable households to improve food availability and income generation.
  • To promote gender equality and protection by safeguarding the rights, safety and dignity of women, girls, children and other vulnerable groups.
  • To improve access to education for children, women, girls and marginalised members of the community.
  • To promote environmental conservation through activities that protect natural resources and encourage sustainable environmental practices.
Our approach

Community-centred and women-led

WIRCO adopts a community-centred, participatory, inclusive, rights-based and women-led approach. We work in partnership with local communities and stakeholders to identify needs, strengthen local capacities and deliver integrated programmes. Through empowerment, protection, collaboration and sustainable development, we seek to build resilient communities where vulnerable and marginalised people can live healthy, safe and dignified lives.

Key implementation strategies

We use practical, community-centred strategies to implement our programmes effectively, reach vulnerable populations and promote sustainable development across South Sudan.

Community mobilisation and engagement

  • Mobilising communities to take part in identifying their needs and priorities
  • Engaging community leaders, elders, women and youth in programme activities
  • Encouraging community ownership of development initiatives

Capacity building and training

  • Providing training for youth, women and community groups
  • Strengthening the skills and knowledge of community members
  • Building the capacity of local structures to manage and sustain development activities

Partnership and networking

  • Collaborating with government ministries, NGOs, UN agencies and development partners
  • Building relationships with local authorities and community-based organisations
  • Promoting information sharing and coordination to avoid duplication of services

Integrated programme implementation

  • Linking health and nutrition interventions with WASH activities
  • Combining food security and livelihood support with women's empowerment
  • Integrating education, gender, protection and environmental conservation into community development programmes

Targeted support to vulnerable groups

  • Prioritising malnourished children, women, girls, older persons and vulnerable families
  • Identifying households and individuals who need special assistance
  • Ensuring fair and inclusive access to programme services

Awareness creation and behaviour change

  • Conducting community awareness campaigns
  • Promoting good nutrition, hygiene, sanitation and environmental protection
  • Educating communities about human rights, gender equality and child protection

Local resource mobilisation

  • Mobilising local resources, skills and knowledge
  • Engaging community volunteers and local structures
  • Seeking financial and material support from donors and development partners

Monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning (MEAL)

  • Regularly monitoring programme activities and progress
  • Measuring results against established objectives and indicators
  • Collecting feedback from programme participants
  • Using lessons learned to improve programme quality and effectiveness

Advocacy and community representation

  • Advocating for the rights and needs of vulnerable communities
  • Engaging policymakers and relevant authorities
  • Promoting policies and practices that protect women, children and marginalised groups

Sustainability and exit strategies

  • Strengthening community ownership of projects
  • Promoting income-generating activities and self-reliance
  • Training local groups to continue activities beyond project funding
  • Encouraging sustainable management of community resources

Working together

A collaborative, multidisciplinary approach

WIRCO brings together a diverse network of partners and stakeholders to strengthen the effectiveness, coordination and impact of our work. Alongside our core team, we work closely with:

Government institutions

National and local government institutions, to ensure alignment with public policies, strengthen institutional coordination and support effective programme implementation at national and community levels.

Community and civil society

Community-based organisations and civil society networks, to promote participatory approaches, amplify local voices and ensure our work responds to the real needs and priorities of communities.

International partners

International NGOs and United Nations agencies, to draw on technical expertise and global good practice, and to strengthen resource mobilisation and programme delivery through strategic partnerships.

By working with these partners, WIRCO aims to build a cohesive, collaborative network that supports sustainable development and long-term impact.
