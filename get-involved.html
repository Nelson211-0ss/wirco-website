Donate

Your gift goes further

Donations go straight into community programs run by and for women. Choose a one-off gift or give monthly to help us plan ahead.

  • $10buys a dignity kit so a girl can attend school every day of the month
  • $25provides seeds and tools for one woman farmer for a full season
  • $50covers a term of school fees, a uniform and books for one girl
  • $100trains a woman mediator to help settle disputes in her village

Volunteer

Share your skills

We welcome volunteers in South Sudan and abroad. Whether you are a trainer, nurse, accountant, designer or writer, your skills can make a real difference.

  • Training and mentoring women's groups
  • Health, counselling and protection support
  • Finance, fundraising and communications
  • Remote support from anywhere in the world
